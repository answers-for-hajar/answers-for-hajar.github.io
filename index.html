02 /Why we’re concerned

Equal protection.
Real accountability.

Our campaign’s position

We believe discrimination may have shaped the response. Racism can appear in decisions and priorities, even when nobody says it out loud. People with an immigrant background should never have to wonder whether their safety matters less.

“I’m sick of the double standards.”

The linked sources do not establish that discrimination caused the initial assessment. That is a concern we want independently examined, alongside the police’s explanation.

  1. Explain the decision

    Set out what information was available, how risk was assessed, and why the initial classification was made.

  2. Independently examine possible bias

    Review whether assumptions about ethnicity, background, or personal circumstances affected the response.

  3. Publish findings. Make changes.

    Provide public findings that respect the family’s privacy, accountability for any failures, and concrete safeguards.

02

Dutch Police

Report suspected discrimination

Anonymous reporting possible
Official channel

The police allow anonymous discrimination reports without personal details. They register the report but do not investigate it through this route. If you report anonymously, they cannot contact you. The form is framed around discrimination you experienced; be clear that you are raising a public concern about this case, not claiming to be a victim or witness.
